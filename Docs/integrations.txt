# DomainDig Integrations

## SMTP submission

Email integrations support implicit TLS ("Direct TLS") on port 465. STARTTLS
on port 587 is not supported.

`SMTPSecurityMode` has two cases: `.plain` and `.directTLS`. Sending with a
username configured and `.plain` selected throws
`IntegrationError.insecureCredentials`, and the integration editor refuses to
save that combination.

Why STARTTLS is not implemented:

- `NWConnection` cannot add TLS to an established connection. The protocol
  stack is fixed when `NWParameters` is constructed, so the plaintext SMTP
  greeting and the `STARTTLS` exchange cannot be followed by a TLS handshake
  on the same connection.
- The alternative is CFStream, which can negotiate TLS in place through
  `kCFStreamPropertySSLSettings`. `CFStreamCreatePairWithSocketToHost` is
  deprecated in favour of Network.framework, which is the API that cannot do
  this. CFStream also has no handshake-completion callback and needs its own
  run-loop thread. A correct implementation must discard any bytes buffered
  before the upgrade; otherwise it is open to the STARTTLS plaintext-injection
  attack (CVE-2011-0411).
- RFC 8314 prefers implicit TLS over STARTTLS for mail submission. It also
  asks that both be offered during the transition, so supporting 465 alone is
  narrower than the RFC wants; the transport constraints above are the reason.
