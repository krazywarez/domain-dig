## Description

DomainDig is a fast, no-frills domain inspection tool for developers, sysadmins, and anyone who manages or troubleshoots servers and infrastructure.

Enter any domain and run a full diagnostic in seconds:

DNS: query A, AAAA, MX, NS, TXT, CNAME, SOA, SRV, CAA, and DS records including wildcard entries, with TTL values, DNSSEC status, and reverse DNS (PTR) lookup for the resolved IP.

SSL/TLS: inspect the full certificate chain — common name, SANs, issuer, validity dates, TLS version and cipher, and days until expiry with color-coded warnings.

Email Security: check SPF, DMARC, DKIM (common selectors), MTA-STS, and BIMI with pass/fail indicators so you can verify email deliverability at a glance.

HTTP Headers: send a HEAD request and review every response header, with security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, and more) highlighted.

Redirect Chain: trace every redirect hop from the first request to the final destination, with the status code at each step.

Reachability & Ports: measure TCP latency on ports 80 and 443, and check common ports (FTP, SSH, SMTP, MySQL, PostgreSQL, and more) to see what's open.

IP Geolocation: see the hosting org, city, region, and country for the resolved IP, on a map.

Ownership & Subdomains: get the registrar, registration and expiry dates, and nameservers via RDAP, check availability, and discover subdomains.

Every check runs in parallel and independently, then rolls up into a risk score with plain-language insights.

Go beyond a single lookup:

- Monitor domains in the background and get alerts when DNS, TLS, or email records change.
- Group domains into Workflows and run them as a batch.
- Compare two domains side by side, and track changes across a snapshot history.
- Run structured Audit sessions with checklists, findings, and evidence.
- Export as text, CSV, JSON, Markdown, or PDF — single reports or whole portfolios.
- Home Screen widgets, Shortcuts, and a share extension put it where you work.
- Optional iCloud sync keeps your domains and history across your devices.
- A built-in local API exposes reports to your own scripts and automation.

Free and paid tiers:
Every check above is free, with up to 5 tracked domains, one workflow, batches of 10, and 50 history snapshots.
Pro removes those limits and adds background monitoring, alerts, advanced exports, and extended subdomain discovery.
Pro+ adds shared portfolios, unlimited audit sessions, the full intelligence breakdown, and uploading reports to your own S3-compatible storage.
Pro and Pro+ are monthly or yearly subscriptions that renew automatically until cancelled in your Apple Account settings.

Local-first by design: your domains, history, and settings stay on your device unless you export, share, or sync them. Lookups go to DNS servers and the domain itself, and, unless you turn enrichment off in Settings, to rdap.org, crt.sh, and ipapi.co.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking.
